# General
Welcome to the Reportnet 3 Help Pages, your go-to resource for everything related to the European Environment Agency’s (EEA) environmental reporting system. Whether you’re a new user or an experienced reporter, this space is designed to help you navigate Reportnet 3 with ease.

### What You’ll Find Here

  * Guides & Tutorials – Step-by-step instructions to help you complete reporting tasks efficiently.
  * FAQs – Answers to common questions about the platform and reporting processes.
  * Best Practices – Tips to improve the quality and consistency of your submissions.
  * Troubleshooting – Solutions to common issues you may encounter.

### Why Reportnet 3?

If you have any questions or need further support, check out our Help Sections or reach out to the [ServiceDesk@eea.europa.eu](mailto:ServiceDesk@eea.europa.eu).

We’re here to make environmental reporting simpler and more effective for you.
