# How to log off
1. On any page, click on the power button in the top right or bottom left of the screen.
  2. By default, you will be asked to confirm you wish to log off,
however this can be turned off in your [global settings](../user-settings/index.md) or in the same pop-up window by clicking “Do not ask again”.
